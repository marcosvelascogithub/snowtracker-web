Source

Generated from the worldwide piste extract of OpenSnowMap (planet_pistes.osm.gz), with OpenStreetMap data. Lines are simplified and tags the app doesn't use are removed. Updated with each app version.

Format

pistes-index.json: extract is the extract date and each item in zones is [min latitude, min longitude, max latitude, max longitude, offset, length]: the zone's bounding box and where its block is in pistes.bin, in bytes.

pistes.bin: the zone blocks, one after another. Each block is a GeoJSON (FeatureCollection of LineString) compressed with xz. Each piste has piste:type and, if present, piste:difficulty and name (its name or, failing that, its piste:name); each lift has aerialway, with the OpenStreetMap values.

resorts.json: extract is the extract date and each item in resorts is [name, name:es, name:en, name:de, name:fr, name:it, name:nl, country in Spanish, country in English, country in German, country in French, country in Italian, country in Dutch, min latitude, min longitude, max latitude, max longitude]: the resort's name in OpenStreetMap, its names in the app's languages, if any, and the bounding box of its pistes and lifts.
