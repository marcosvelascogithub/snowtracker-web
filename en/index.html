Live

Every run, as it happens.

Tap Start and forget about it. SnowTracker tells runs from lifts on its own and shows which run you’re on, how fast you’re going, how much vertical you’ve skied and the slope of the run. With your iPhone locked, the session stays on the Lock Screen, with Pause close at hand.

The map

Your whole day, on the map.

The day’s route, colored by speed on Apple Maps, with every run numbered. Tap one and the map frames it. And if you want to relive it, play it back up to 60 times faster.

The summary

The whole day, at a glance.

Max and average speed, vertical, distance skiing and on lifts, every run with its speed and the day’s altitude profile. Sort your runs by number or by speed.

Today

It knows which resort you’re at.

When you open the app, Today tells you which resort you’re at, what you’ve done so far today and how your season is going. Choose ski, snowboard or telemark and start.

The season

All your vertical adds up.

Days, runs, distance and the total vertical of the whole season, compared with the previous one. Your records, each with its day and resort, and every resort where you’ve skied.

Apple Watch

You ski, your watch keeps up.

Start on your iPhone and keep it in your pocket. On your wrist you see the run you’re on, your speed, your heart rate and your vertical, and you pause or lock the screen from your wrist. Each day’s heart rate and calories stay in its summary.

And more

Made for the mountains.

On the Lock Screen

The session in a Live Activity, with the run, the speed and the time, and Pause without opening the app.

You never lose a session

If the app closes or your iPhone turns off, the session is recovered when you come back. And it lets you know when the battery is running low.

Save to Health

Each day, as a workout in the Health app, with the route and, with the watch, your heart rate and calories.

Share the route

Export any day’s route as GPX to use it in other apps.

Ski, snowboard or telemark

Choose your activity; the stats are measured the same way.

Your units

Kilometers or miles, meters or feet, depending on your region, and VoiceOver reads each number with its unit.

Privacy

Your data, on your iPhone.

SnowTracker has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your sessions are stored on your device and nobody else sees them.
