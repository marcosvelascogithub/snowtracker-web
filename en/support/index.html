Help in the app

In the app, in Settings › Help, you’ll find the most common questions: how to start, pause and end a session, what Today and Season show, how to use Apple Watch and how to save your sessions to Health.

Privacy

SnowTracker has no accounts and no servers: your sessions are stored on your device. More in the privacy policy.
